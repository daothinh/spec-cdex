# Protocol Archetypes

Use the target's dominant archetype to prioritize invariants.

## Token / Vesting / Escrow

- prioritize mint, burn, permit, vesting release, rescue, and escrow release paths

## AMM / DEX Pool

- prioritize reserve invariants, fees, callbacks, slippage, and oracle accumulation

## Vault / Yield Strategy

- prioritize share accounting, strategy debt, harvest flows, and keeper trust

## Lending / Borrowing

- prioritize collateral math, debt accounting, liquidation, and oracle freshness

## Staking / Rewards

- prioritize reward checkpoints, delegation, unstake timing, and emissions accounting

## Bridge / Messaging

- prioritize proof verification, replay protection, relayer trust, and unlock or mint flows

## Governance / Timelock

- prioritize voting power snapshots, queueing, execution, and emergency powers

## Perps / Orderbook / Exchange

- prioritize margin, liquidation, funding, settlement, sequencing, and custody boundaries

## NFT / Marketplace

- prioritize order auth, royalties, settlement, and replay

## Oracle Consumer

- prioritize feed freshness, decimals, bounds, and fallback logic
